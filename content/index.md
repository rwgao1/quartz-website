I'm currently a master's student in CS at [Brown University](https://cs.brown.edu/) working with Prof. [George Konidaris](https://cs.brown.edu/people/gdk/) and Prof. [Randall Balestriero](https://randallbalestriero.github.io/). Previously, I obtained my bachelor's in CS from [UCSB](https://www.cs.ucsb.edu/). There, I worked on zeroth-order optimization and tensor-compressed learning, advised by Prof. [Zheng Zhang](https://web.ece.ucsb.edu/~zhengzhang/).

Right now, I'm interested in *model-based reinforcement learning and state abstraction*. Currently researching ways to use learned models for decision-time policy improvement.
